01 / About

About the Conference

This gathering combines two events: the 8th Meeting of Nigerian Members and Potential Members of the International Association for Community Development (IACD), and the 7th National Consultative Conference/Workshop on Community and Regional Development in Nigeria.

It is organised by IACD and the Institute of Community Development Practitioners (ICDP), Nigeria, linking international community development practice with Nigeria’s national and regional priorities.

Goal

Conference Goal

Provide a platform for stakeholders to discuss emerging issues, share experiences, and develop innovative strategies for regional development in Nigeria.
